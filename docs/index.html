What the data show

The catalog starts late

The first record is from April 16 at 23:47 local time, almost five hours after the mainshock. The most numerous aftershocks of those hours are missing, and that biases Omori and Gutenberg-Richter.

April has no nearest city

385 of the 391 events from April 2016 carry nas as the nearest city. The original script drops them before the centrality analysis, right in the most active period.

Fixed depth of 10 km

266 events have exactly 10 km, the default value of the network. They should be flagged or excluded in any analysis that relies on depth.

Two weak clusters

Cluster 3 has a silhouette close to zero and cluster 5 is negative. They look more like fragments of their neighbors than like seismic zones of their own.
